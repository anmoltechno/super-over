Super Over support

Quick-fire cricket for iPhone, iPad and the browser.

Play in your browser

Batting

Swipe where you want the ball to go, as it reaches the bat. A gentle swipe keeps it on the ground; flick hard to loft it. Read the length: pull the short ones, drive the full ones. The timing readout after each shot tells you if you were early or late.

Bowling

Pick a delivery, drag the target to where it should pitch, and let go to bowl. Tap as the ring closes on the target for pinpoint accuracy. Between overs, choose who bowls next and set an attacking, balanced or defensive field.

Ways to play

  • World Tour: thirty stops across six grounds, three stars each.
  • Quick Match: pick the overs, wickets, ground and opponent, then toss.
  • Super Over: one over each, against the CPU or a friend on one device.
  • Blitz: bat until you're out.
  • Daily Chase: the same target for everyone, every day.
  • Nets: practise batting or bowling against pace, swing or spin.

Questions

Where is my progress saved? On your device: inside the app, or in your browser's storage for this site in the browser version. Nothing is sent anywhere, so progress doesn't move between devices.

There's no sound in the browser. Browsers start sound only after you touch the page: tap anywhere once. Check that Sound is on in the menu and your device isn't muted.

It's slow on my computer. Close other heavy tabs; the game draws every frame on the canvas and runs best in an up-to-date Safari, Chrome, Edge or Firefox.

How do I use iPhone Duo's fold? In the app, fold it like a laptop: the broadcast stands up behind the fold and the whole ground lies flat under your thumbs. Fold it like a book for the field map on one page and the broadcast on the other.
